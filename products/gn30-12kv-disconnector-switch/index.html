Product Overview

GN30-12kV is an indoor rotary disconnector for 12 kV, 50 Hz systems, with rated-current versions from 630 A to 3150 A. Two groups of insulators and contacts are mounted above and below a common three-phase frame, and rotating contacts open or close the circuit. GN30-12D models add an earthing blade.

Rotary Contact Construction

The contacts rotate within an upper-and-lower arrangement on a shared frame. This layout keeps the switch compact for installation in indoor switchgear.

No-Load Circuit Isolation

The disconnector provides circuit isolation when no load current is flowing. Select it for isolation duty and coordinate operation with the circuit breaker or other load-switching device.

Key Features

630-3150 A Current Options

The series includes 630, 1000, 1250, 2000, 2500 and 3150 A versions. Match the current rating and corresponding short-time and peak withstand ratings to the circuit.

D-Type Earthing Versions

GN30-12D adds an earthing blade to the standard GN30-12 switch. Specify the earthing arrangement with the required current version and switchgear layout.

Switchgear Or Separate Installation

The rotary disconnector can be installed with indoor switchgear or used separately. Match the support, operating linkage and terminal positions to the installation.

Standard And Earthing Configurations

GN30-12 Standard Version

The standard GN30-12 version uses rotary contacts to make or break the isolation gap. Choose the rated-current version to suit the circuit and installation space.

GN30-12D Earthing Version

GN30-12D includes an additional earthing blade. Confirm the blade arrangement, operating sequence and required interlocks with the selected assembly.

Installation And Operating Interfaces

Mounting And Terminals

Use the GN30-12(D) outline and installation drawing matching the rated current and earthing version. Check the mounting surface, terminal positions and available installation space before selection.

Operating Linkage

Specify the operating mechanism and linkage arrangement with the switch. Check the mounting surface, terminal connections and full contact travel against the switchgear layout.

Service Conditions

Ambient Temperature

-10°C to +40°C for the general indoor configuration.

Altitude

Not exceeding 1000 m above sea level.

Relative Humidity

Daily average not exceeding 95%; monthly average not exceeding 90%.

Water-Vapour Pressure

Daily average up to 2.2 kPa; monthly average up to 1.8 kPa.

Cold-Region Arrangements

The manual lists a -25°C lower limit for cold-region arrangements. Confirm this requirement separately from the general indoor configuration.

Installation Environment

Indoor locations without fire or explosion hazards, serious pollution, chemical corrosion or severe vibration.

Specify Your GN30-12kV Disconnector

Send the rated current and required fault withstand ratings. Include the earthing requirement, switchgear layout, operating linkage and available installation space so the correct model and drawing can be selected.
