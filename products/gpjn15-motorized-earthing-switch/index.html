Product Overview

GPJN15 Motorized Type is an indoor AC earthing switch for 12, 24 and 40.5 kV switchgear in three-phase, 50/60 Hz systems. It provides the earthing function during maintenance, with a built-in motor drive for opening and closing and provision for manual operation. The series includes 31.5 kA versions at all three voltage levels, plus 40 kA and 50 kA versions at 12 kV.

Motorized Operation

The drive assembly acts on the earthing-switch shaft in both directions. Select the AC or DC control supply to suit the switchgear control circuit.

Manual Operation Retained

The mechanism also allows manual operation. Cabinet integration requires the connecting rod and interlock parts matched to the motorized arrangement.

Key Features

Motor-Driven Opening And Closing

A motor, reducer, bevel gears and control board form the drive assembly. Reversing the drive turns the operating shaft to open or close the earthing switch.

Spring Closing Mechanism

The spring stores and releases energy as the mechanism passes its dead point, driving the earthing knives into the fixed contacts. The mechanism supports both manual and motorized operation.

AC And DC Control Supplies

Control supply options are AC 220 V, DC 220 V and DC 110 V. Specify the required supply when ordering and match it to the cabinet control circuit.

Interlocking And Cabinet Integration

Matched Connecting Rod

Use the GreenPower connecting rod matched to the motorized mechanism. The linkage differs from the manual arrangement; an unsuitable rod can overload and damage the motor.

Rear-Door Interlock

Where rear-door interlocking is required, use the matched rear-door mechanism and travel switch. The motor operating supply must be interrupted while the rear door is open.

Electrical Interlocking

Use auxiliary contacts from the associated electrical equipment to interrupt the earthing-switch operating supply when operation is blocked by the interlock circuit.

Service Conditions

Altitude

Not exceeding 1000 m above sea level.

Ambient Temperature

-25°C to +45°C.

Relative Humidity

Daily average below 95%; monthly average below 90%.

Seismic Intensity

Not exceeding degree 8.

Installation Environment

Keep the installation free from corrosive or explosive gases, vapours and deposits, and from dust or contamination that can impair insulation.

Vibration

The installation location must be free from frequent severe vibration.
