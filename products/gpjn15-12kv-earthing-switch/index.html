Product Overview

GPJN15-12kV is an assembled indoor high-voltage AC earthing switch for three-phase, 50/60 Hz power systems. It provides earthing protection during switchgear maintenance and has short-circuit making capability. The series includes 31.5 kA, 40 kA and 50 kA short-time withstand versions, with model-specific durations, phase spacings and installation arrangements.

Maintenance Earthing

Installed as the earthing function in high-voltage switchgear, the switch supports the grounding arrangements required for equipment maintenance.

Three Withstand Versions

Choose 31.5 kA for 4 s, 40 kA for 4 s or 50 kA for 3 s. Each version has its own making-current rating and installation options.

Key Features

Short-Circuit Making Capacity

Making-current ratings of 80, 100 and 125 kA correspond to the 31.5, 40 and 50 kA withstand versions. Select the combination required by the switchgear fault duty.

Phase Spacing Options

Multiple phase spacings support different switchgear layouts. Available spacings vary by withstand version, so confirm the model and mounting arrangement together.

Live Indicator Options

The earthing switch can be ordered with or without a live indicator device. Specify the required indicator model when this option is needed.

Model Selection And Installation

The model suffix identifies phase spacing in millimetres. For example, GPJN15-12/31.5-275 identifies the 12 kV, 31.5 kA version with 275 mm phase spacing.

Left And Right Operating Arrangements

Determine the operating direction while facing the product with the insulators above and the movable blades below. Confirm this reference orientation before specifying the shaft side.

Left Operating

With the product viewed in the reference orientation, the main shaft on your left identifies the left-operating arrangement. Specify this direction in the order.

Right Operating

With the product viewed in the same reference orientation, the main shaft on your right identifies the right-operating arrangement. Check the required shaft side against the switchgear layout.
