Product Overview

ZW7-40.5kV is an outdoor vacuum circuit breaker for substations and overhead lines in 33-40.5 kV power systems. The series offers rated currents from 630 A to 2500 A and short-circuit breaking ratings of 20 kA, 25 kA and 31.5 kA. Conventional interrupter assemblies and embedded-pole designs are available, with porcelain or silicone rubber external insulation according to the selected variant.

Substations And Overhead Lines

The breaker switches load current and interrupts overload and short-circuit current in three-phase, 50 Hz systems. Applications include utility substations, power plants and industrial or mining networks.

Optional Intelligent Control

An FTU controller, voltage transformer and metering equipment can be combined with the breaker for protection, automatic reclosing, remote control and energy metering.

Key Features

95 / 185 kV Insulation

The series is rated for 95 kV power-frequency withstand for one minute and 185 kV lightning impulse withstand.

Protection And Metering CTs

Up to three current transformers can be fitted, with up to four cores per CT. Ratios, accuracy classes and burdens are selected for the protection and metering circuits.

Rain And Condensation Control

The mechanism enclosure uses drainage grooves and foam door seals. A temperature controller and heater help limit condensation inside the enclosure.

Operating Mechanisms

Spring Mechanism

The standard arrangement uses a spring operating mechanism; the manual identifies the CT19BW mechanism. Opening, closing and motor energy-storage supplies can be specified at 110 V or 220 V, AC or DC.

Permanent-Magnet Option

The model designation and ordering schedule also include an M-type permanent-magnet mechanism. Specify this option with its operating power supply and control interface.

Service Conditions

Ambient Temperature

-30°C to +40°C; the 24-hour average must not exceed +35°C.

Standard Altitude

Not exceeding 1000 m. Higher-altitude equipment requires a specified configuration.

Humidity

Relative humidity: daily average up to 95%, monthly average up to 90%. Water-vapour pressure: daily average up to 2.2 kPa, monthly average up to 1.8 kPa.

Wind And Ice

Wind speed up to 34 m/s; ice thickness up to 20 mm.

Pollution And Seismic Conditions

Pollution level II under normal conditions; seismic intensity not exceeding degree 8.

Special Site Conditions

Higher pollution, severe condensation, salt spray and extreme temperatures require review before ordering.

Installation And High-Altitude Selection

High-Altitude Configurations

The manual describes configurations for 2000 m, 3000 m, 4000 m and 5000 m. These require changes to the selected structure and insulation clearances; the full altitude range is not shared by every variant.

Mounting And Foundation

Mounting arrangements and foundation dimensions differ by structure and altitude version. Confirm the complete assembly, support, terminal positions and foundation drawing before site work.

The catalog includes dimensioned drawings for the individual variants. Download it for reference and request the final drawing for the ordered configuration.
