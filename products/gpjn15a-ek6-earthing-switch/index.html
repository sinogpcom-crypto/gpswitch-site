Product Overview

GPJN15A EK6, model GPJN15A-12/31.5, is an assembled indoor high-voltage AC earthing switch for three-phase, 50/60 Hz systems in the 3–12 kV range. It provides the earthing function during switchgear maintenance and has short-circuit making capability. Its rated voltage is 12 kV, with 31.5 kA short-time withstand current for 4 s and a mechanical life of 3,000 operations.

Maintenance Earthing

Installed as the earthing function in high-voltage switchgear, the switch supports the grounding arrangements required for equipment maintenance.

12 kV Three-Phase Systems

Designed for indoor AC power systems at 50 or 60 Hz. Match the phase spacing, operating direction and mounting dimensions to the selected cabinet.

Key Features

80 kA Making Capacity

The GPJN15A-12/31.5 model has an 80 kA short-circuit making rating and an 80 kA peak withstand rating, alongside its 31.5 kA, 4 s short-time withstand duty.

3,000-Operation Mechanical Life

Mechanical life is rated at 3,000 operations. Include the expected operating duty in technical selection for the switchgear installation.

Live Indicator Options

The switch can be ordered with or without a live indicator device. Specify the required indicator model when this option is needed.
