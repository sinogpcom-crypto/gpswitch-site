Product Overview

GPR1-40.5kV is a compact, modular SF6 gas insulated ring main unit, also described as box type gas insulated metal enclosed switchgear. It supports ring network and dual radial power supply for 40.5kV cable distribution systems. Load switch, switch-fuse and vacuum circuit breaker units can operate independently or be combined into a complete distribution scheme, including compact arrangements for box type substations and wind power collection networks.

Sealed Primary System

The high voltage primary components are enclosed in a laser-welded stainless steel gas tank filled with SF6. The sealed design isolates live parts from the surrounding operating environment.

Modular And Compact Arrangements

Choose individual functional units or a compact common-tank combination. Plug-in busbar connectors support left, right or double-sided extension according to the selected cabinet configuration.

Key Features

Compact Footprint

Standard C, F and 630A V units are 430 mm wide, 1000 mm deep and 1700 mm high, helping fit secondary distribution equipment into limited installation space.

Stainless Steel Gas Tank

The sealed gas tank uses 3 mm stainless steel plate and laser welding. Automatic helium leak detection supports gas tightness and long service life.

Vacuum Circuit Breaking

The V unit combines a vacuum circuit breaker with a 3-position switch. Its rated short-circuit breaking current is 25 kA and circuit breaker mechanical life is 10,000 operations.

Plug-In Busbar Extension

Cabinets connect through insulated plug-in busbar connectors. Cabinet assembly and extension do not require on-site handling of SF6 gas.

Protected High Voltage Compartments

The gas tank and fuse compartment have IP67 protection. The low voltage control compartment has IP3X protection.

Mechanical And Electrical Interlocks

Interlocks coordinate switching, isolation, earthing and access to the cable compartment to help prevent dangerous misoperation.

Independent Pressure Relief

Each gas tank has a pressure relief device that directs pressure flow toward the top or rear of the cabinet in the event of an internal fault.

Protection And Control Options

Motorized operation, gas density monitoring, current transformers, fault indicators and active or self-powered protection relays can be selected for the required scheme.

Functional Units And Protection

C - Load Switch Unit

A 630A unit for incoming and outgoing feeder switching. It includes a spring-operated load switch, an earthing switch and mechanical interlocking, with motorized load switch operation available as an option.

F - Switch-Fuse Unit

Combines a load switch and high breaking capacity fuse for transformer control and protection up to 2000 kVA. Dual earthing arrangements allow both sides of the fuse to be earthed for replacement.

V - Vacuum Circuit Breaker Unit

Available in 630A and 1250A versions for feeder, motor and transformer protection. Active or self-powered relays provide selectable overcurrent, short-circuit and earth-fault protection functions.

Busbar And Cable Units

IL/IR busbar switch units, RB busbar riser units and RCL/RCR cable incoming units support sectionalizing and cable connection schemes. Compact common-tank arrangements include CC, CV, CCV, CCC and CCF.

Ambient Conditions

Ambient Temperature

-25°C to +40°C, with a maximum daily average temperature of +35°C.

Altitude

Up to 1000 m under normal service conditions. Higher altitude applications require technical confirmation.

Relative Humidity

Daily average ≤ 95%; monthly average ≤ 90%. Daily and monthly average water vapor pressure limits are 2.2 kPa and 1.8 kPa respectively.

Installation Environment

Normal service conditions exclude significant dust, smoke, corrosive or combustible gases, vapor and salt mist. Special climatic or pollution conditions should be agreed during project review.

Outdoor And Automation Options

Outdoor Switch Stations

The series can be arranged in an outdoor switch station or box type substation. Select the enclosure, cable access, site ventilation and environmental provisions for the installation conditions.

Local And Remote Operation

Motorized mechanisms and a low voltage control compartment support local or remote switching. Gas density relays, protection relays, current transformers and fault indicators are available according to the required control scheme.
