Product Overview

GPWF1-24kV is a pole-mounted SF6 load break switch for 24 kV, 630 A distribution networks operating at 50 or 60 Hz. It switches load and overload current on outdoor feeders, with manual and motorized operation through a local or remote controller. The assembly includes a sealed gas tank, silicone rubber bushings, a gas pressure gauge and mechanical position indication.

Outdoor Feeder Switching

The GPWF1-24/630 makes and breaks load and overload current in distribution networks. Its 630 A load breaking rating is separate from its 20 kA / 4 s short-time withstand rating.

Stainless-Steel Control Enclosure

The electronic controller is housed in a stainless-steel enclosure and connected to the switch tank by control and power cables. Manual, motorized, local and remote operation are supported.

Key Features

Sealed SF6 Switching Tank

SF6 provides insulation and arc quenching inside the sealed tank. The specified filling pressure is 0.07 MPa at 20°C.

Manual And Motorized Operation

The switch can be operated manually or by its 24 V DC drive. The controller provides local and remote operation for outdoor feeder switching.

Visible Position And Gas Pressure

The switch body includes open/close indication, a manual blocking device and a gas pressure gauge. Control-box lamps provide a separate status indication.

SF6 Gas And Pressure Protection

Gas Quality And Sealing

The annual relative SF6 leakage rate is specified at no more than 0.5%. Moisture limits are 150 ppm at factory delivery and 300 ppm in service.

Pressure Alarm And Relief

The manual lists a low-pressure lockout and alarm range of 0.02-0.04 MPa, and a high-pressure relief device operating range of 0.4-0.6 MPa. The normal rated operating pressure is 0.07 MPa.

Check the pressure gauge against the specified gas conditions and temperature-pressure curve before operation. Use the installation and operation manual for pressure checks and servicing.

Installation And Control Connections

Pole-Mounting Interfaces

The outline drawing shows 300 mm phase spacing and 650 × 390 mm base mounting centres. Confirm the support arrangement, pole fittings and terminal clearances using the final installation drawing.

Control And Position Indication

Specify the 24 V DC operating supply and the local/remote control connections. During commissioning, check that the mechanical open/close indication agrees with the control-box lamps.

The catalog includes outline dimensions, a secondary schematic and pole-mounting diagrams. Confirm the control-cable pin assignments and mounting hardware for the supplied assembly.

Service Conditions

Altitude

Not exceeding 3000 m above sea level.

Ambient Temperature

-40°C to +50°C, with a maximum daily temperature variation of 25°C.

Relative Humidity

Daily average not exceeding 95%; monthly average not exceeding 90%.

Wind And Seismic Conditions

Wind speed up to 34 m/s; seismic intensity not exceeding degree 8.

Ice, Snow And Sun

Ice thickness below 10 mm; snow thickness below 35 mm; solar irradiance below 0.1 W/cm².

Pollution And Installation Site

Pollution Class III or IV. The site must be free from fire and explosion hazards, serious chemical corrosion and frequent severe vibration.
