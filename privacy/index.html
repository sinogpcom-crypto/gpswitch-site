Privacy Policy

How we collect, use and protect information submitted through gpswitch.com.

Zhejiang Greenpower I&E Co., Ltd. respects your privacy. This policy explains how we collect, use and protect information submitted through gpswitch.com.

1. Who We Are

Zhejiang Greenpower I&E Co., Ltd. operates this website and handles international inquiries and export-related business under the GreenPower Electric brand.

Zhejiang Greenpower Electric Co., Ltd. is our affiliated manufacturing company and supports customer projects with manufacturing and technical expertise.

2. Information We Collect

When you send an inquiry, request a catalogue, or contact us by email or WhatsApp, we may collect:

  • Your name, company name, country and contact details
  • Your email address, telephone number or WhatsApp number
  • Product interests, technical requirements and project information
  • Information contained in messages or documents you send to us

We may also collect basic technical information, such as IP address, browser type, device type, referring page, visited pages, approximate location and website usage data.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Respond to inquiries and provide quotations
  • Recommend suitable products and technical solutions
  • Provide catalogues, documents and technical support
  • Follow up on active projects and customer requests
  • Improve our website, products and services
  • Protect the website against misuse and security risks
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations

We use this information to respond to your request and prepare for a possible contract. We may also rely on our legitimate business interests, your consent where required, or a legal obligation.

We do not sell or rent your personal information. We will not send bulk marketing emails or newsletters unless you have specifically agreed to receive them.

4. Service Providers and International Processing

We may share relevant inquiry and project information between Zhejiang Greenpower I&E Co., Ltd. and Zhejiang Greenpower Electric Co., Ltd. when necessary to prepare quotations, provide technical support, manufacture products or follow up on projects.

We also use service providers to operate the website and process requests, including Formspree for inquiry submissions, Google Apps Script and Google Sheets for catalogue requests, Google Analytics for website usage measurement, and GitHub Pages for website hosting.

Your information may therefore be processed in China or other countries where these companies or service providers operate. Data protection standards may differ between countries. Where required by applicable law, we take steps intended to provide appropriate protection. We may also disclose information when required by law or reasonably necessary to protect our legal rights.

5. Cookies and Analytics

The website uses technology required to function and may use optional analytics to understand how visitors use it.

Where required by law, we ask for consent before using optional analytics cookies or similar storage. You can also control or delete cookies through your browser settings.

6. Retention and Security

Inquiry and catalogue-request records are generally kept for up to 24 months after our last relevant communication with you. Information may be retained longer when an active project, contract or legal obligation requires it.

Catalogue-request details stored on your device are kept locally for up to 30 days. We use reasonable safeguards to protect personal information, but no internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure.

7. Your Rights and Contact

Depending on the law that applies to you, you may request access to, correction of or deletion of your personal information, or object to certain uses. Where processing is based on consent, you may withdraw that consent.

For privacy questions or requests, contact gp@sinogp.com.

If you have concerns, you may also contact the data protection authority in your country or region.

We may update this policy when our website, services or legal obligations change. The latest version will be published on this page.
