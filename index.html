What We Supply

Our Business

Medium-voltage switchgear systems, vacuum circuit breakers, RMUs, and components from a China-based manufacturer.

01

Metal-Clad Switchgear

Removable metal-clad switchgear for 12kV to 40.5kV indoor applications, built for protection, control, and reliable power distribution.
02

Ring Main Unit

SF6, dry air, and eco-friendly RMU solutions for 12kV and 24kV distribution networks and compact substations.
03

Vacuum Circuit Breaker

Indoor vacuum circuit breakers from 12kV to 40.5kV, compatible with GreenPower and major international brand switchgear.
04

Load Break And Isolating Switches

Outdoor and indoor load break and isolating switches for 12kV to 40.5kV feeder control, sectionalizing, and network reconfiguration.
05

Instrument Transformers And Indicators

Current and voltage transformers, charged display indicators, and test tools for 12kV to 40.5kV metering, protection, and diagnostics.
06

Switchgear Components And Spare Parts

Chassis, insulating parts, contacts, operating mechanisms, and accessories for 12kV to 40.5kV switchgear retrofit, maintenance, and new builds.
